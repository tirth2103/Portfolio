## How to View Your Analytics

### Step 1: Go to Google Analytics
Visit: https://analytics.google.com/

### Step 2: Sign In
Use the same Google account you used to create the tracking ID

### Step 3: Select Your Property
- Click on your property (should be your portfolio website)
- You'll see the main dashboard

## Important Dashboards to Check

### 1. **Real-Time Report** (See Who's Online NOW!)
- Left sidebar → Reports → Realtime
- Shows visitors currently on your site
- See what pages they're viewing in real-time

### 2. **User Acquisition**
- Reports → Acquisition → User acquisition
- See where your visitors come from (Google, LinkedIn, direct, etc.)

### 3. **Pages and Screens**
- Reports → Engagement → Pages and screens
- See which pages are most popular
- Track time spent on each page

### 4. **Demographics**
- Reports → User → Demographics
- Age, gender, interests of your visitors
- Geographic location

### 5. **Events**
- Reports → Engagement → Events
- See your custom events:
  - `download` - Resume downloads
  - `form_submit` - Contact form submissions
  - `click` - Button clicks (LinkedIn, Get In Touch)

### 6. **Tech Details**
- Reports → Tech → Tech details
- See devices, browsers, operating systems

## How to Create Custom Reports

1. Click "Explore" in left sidebar
2. Click "Blank" to create custom report
3. Drag and drop metrics you want to see
4. Save for future use

## Troubleshooting

**Not seeing data?**
1. Wait 24-48 hours for reports to populate
2. Check Realtime report first (immediate data)
3. Make sure you deployed the latest changes to tirthc.in

**Want to exclude your own visits?**
1. Install "Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on"
2. Or filter by IP address in GA settings
